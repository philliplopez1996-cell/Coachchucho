FAQs

How do I add a team?▼
Go to the Roster tab and tap "+ New Team". Each team is a folder that holds its own set of player cards.
How do I add or edit players?▼
Open a team folder and tap "+ Add Player", or tap any existing player card to view it, then tap Edit to change their name, number, position, photo, and attribute ratings.
How are attributes and OVR calculated?▼
Each attribute is rated 0–99. Overall (OVR) is the average of all attribute ratings, just like a FIFA-style player card.
Can I customize the attributes?▼
Yes. Open Coach Profile from the side menu and use the Attributes section to add new stats or delete ones you don't use. Changes apply to every player card and the radar chart.
How does Roster Radar work?▼
Pick any two players from any team to see an overlaid radar chart plus a side-by-side breakdown of every attribute, with the higher value highlighted.
How do I build a lineup on the Tactical Pitch?▼
Select a team, pick a formation to auto-lay-out your starters, then drag any player chip to reposition them or drag from the bench to bring a substitute on. Tap "Save Formation" to keep it.
Is my data saved?▼
Yes — everything is saved to your coach account on the server, so it's there whenever you log back in from any device.
