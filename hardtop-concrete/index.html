Primary Specialty

Specialized Infrastructure
& Utility Work

Hard Top Concrete handles some of the most technically demanding concrete and utility work in the industry — critical power infrastructure, data center build-outs, and underground utility corridors, anywhere in the country.

Backup Generator Rough-Ins

Site preparation, excavation, conduit rough-in, and concrete pads for backup generator installations at industrial, commercial, and mission-critical facilities.

Data Center Concrete Infrastructure

Precision concrete support structures, raised floor pads, and critical flatwork for data center environments where tolerances matter.

Underground Utility Excavation & Conduit

Trenching, excavation, and installation of underground electrical conduit for utility distribution and power delivery systems.

Equipment Pads

Engineered concrete pads for generators, switchgear, BESS (battery energy storage systems), and transformers — sized, reinforced, and poured to load spec.

Duct Bank Encasement

Concrete encasement of bundled conduit systems — formed, reinforced, and poured to electrical and civil specifications for long-term underground utility protection.

Flatwork

Foundations

Parking Lots, Driveways & Sidewalks

High-traffic surfaces built for longevity — parking areas, driveways, access roads, and pedestrian paths.

Stamped Concrete

Decorative stamped finishes for patios, pool decks, and driveways — texture and pattern that holds up to real use.

Concrete Saw Cutting
