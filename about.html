About

Telling stories,
one frame at a time.

I picked up a camera years ago and haven't put it down since. What started as a way to hold on to moments turned into a way of seeing — chasing light, watching how people move through a scene, waiting for the second before or after the obvious shot.

Over the years my work has settled into a few things I keep coming back to: portraits that let people be exactly who they are, landscapes that make you feel small in a good way, and the unplanned, in-between moments you catch on the street or on the road.

This site is a collection of that work — a running record of what's caught my eye, camera in hand, over the years.
