Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

What age groups do you coach?▼
I coach players from U-6 through U-16. Whether your child is just learning or training competitively, I have programs tailored to their level.
Do I need to do anything before my first session?▼
Yes — every new player starts with a one-time $30 evaluation session. It's required before booking any 1-on-1 or Small Group sessions and helps build a training plan suited to your player.
Can my child train with friends?▼
Yes! Small Group Training lets 2–4 players train together for $80 per session, split among the group — a more budget-friendly way to get quality coaching.
How long are sessions?▼
One session is 75 minutes. Sessions are flexible and billed in 15-minute increments as needed.
Where do sessions take place?▼
Sessions are held at two main field locations in the area. You'll select your preferred field when booking.
Can I buy sessions to use later?▼
Yes! The 5-session and 10-session plans don't expire so you can use them at your own pace.
What if my child is a beginner?▼
No problem! I work with all skill levels and will create a personalized training plan for your child.
